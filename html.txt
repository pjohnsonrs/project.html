  <h1>Chocolate Fudge</h1>

  <img
    src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1575377427642-087cf684f29d"
    alt="Pieces of delicious chocolate fudge"
    width="400"

  <h2>Description</h2>

  <p>Chocolate fudge is a rich, creamy, and delicious dessert.</p>
  <p>It is easy to make and is perfect for sharing with family and friends.</p>

  <h2>Ingredients</h2>

  <ul>
    <li>3 cups of chocolate chips</li>
    <li>1 can of sweetened condensed milk</li>
    <li>1 teaspoon of vanilla extract</li>
    <li>A pinch of salt</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Steps</h2>

  <ol>
    <li>Melt the chocolate chips over low heat.</li>
    <li>Stir in the condensed milk, vanilla extract, and salt.</li>
    <li>Pour the mixture into a prepared baking dish.</li>
    <li>Allow the fudge to cool before cutting it into pieces.</li>
  </ol>
